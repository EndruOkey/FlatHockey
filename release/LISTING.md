## Dlouhý popis (EN)
FlatHockey is a physics-based top-down ice hockey game you play right in your browser.
No download, no account — open it and you are on the ice.

- Real skating feel: momentum, glide and hockey stops
- Stick control with the mouse: deke, sweep, and pick your corner
- Charged shots — from a quick wrist shot to a full slapshot
- One-timers, passes, deflections and body checks
- Online lobbies for up to 10 players — and a bot fills the empty spots, so you never wait
- Interactive tutorial and training scenarios

## Ovládání (EN)
- **W A S D / arrows** — skate
- **Mouse** — aim the stick (farther cursor = longer reach)
- **Left mouse** — hold to charge, release to shoot
- **Right mouse** — pass to the nearest teammate
- **Middle mouse** — call for a pass
- **Space** — hockey stop (hold)
